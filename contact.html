Visit us

Cwm Aeron Farm

Four miles south of Hay-on-Wye, up a lane that your satnav will insist is not a road. It is a road. Keep going.

Farm shop

Friday to Sunday, 9am – 4pm. Veg, eggs, apple juice, and saplings in season.

Open farm day

First Saturday of the month, 11am. Free, two hours, no booking needed.

Getting here

Bus 39 from Hereford stops at Glasbury, two miles away. We will collect you if you ask.

Accessibility

Level access to the shop, barn and riverside trail. Accessible WC on site.
