Accessibility statement

Last reviewed 1 August 2026. This covers both the website and visiting the farm itself.

How accessible this website is

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. In practice that means:

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a skip link to the main content.
  • Text contrast meets or exceeds 4.5:1 in both the light and dark themes.
  • All motion — scroll reveals, parallax, marquees, page transitions — is switched off automatically when your device requests reduced motion.
  • Text resizes to 200% without loss of content, and the layout reflows to a single column on narrow screens.
  • Images that carry meaning have alternative text; decorative illustrations are hidden from screen readers.
  • Forms use real labels, describe their errors in text as well as colour, and announce success politely.

What we know is not perfect yet

  • The project carousel can be scrolled with arrow buttons and a trackpad, but does not yet respond to arrow keys when focus is inside the track.
  • Our downloadable impact report PDF is not fully tagged. We will publish an HTML version alongside it in the 2026 edition.
  • Some data tables are wide and scroll horizontally on small screens.

These are on our list, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise.

Visiting the farm

  • Level, firm-surfaced access to the farm shop, the barn and the first 400m of the riverside trail.
  • An accessible WC with grab rails and a level threshold, next to the shop.
  • Designated parking within 20m of the shop entrance.
  • Assistance dogs are welcome everywhere on the farm.
  • Seated planting stations at every volunteer day, on level ground, with raised beds for nursery work.
  • Black Hill and the upper Aeron plots are steep, rough and not currently wheelchair accessible. We will always find you an alternative site on the same day.

Tell us when we get it wrong

If something on this site or at the farm is difficult to use, email access@rootstock.earth or call +44 (0)1497 555 118. We aim to reply within two working days and to fix website issues within 30 days.

If you need any information from this site in another format — large print, plain text, audio or an easy-read summary — ask and we will send it within ten working days, free.

Enforcement

If you are not happy with how we respond, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS). We take that route seriously and would much rather resolve it with you directly first.
