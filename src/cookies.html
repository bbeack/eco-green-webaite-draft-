Cookie policy

Last updated 1 August 2026. There is no cookie banner on this site, because there is nothing to consent to.

{{icon:check-circle:16}} We set no advertising cookies, no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics. Everything below is stored in your own browser and never sent to us.

What we store, and why

NameTypePurposeExpires
rootstock:themeLocal storageRemembers whether you chose the light or dark theme.Until you clear it
rootstock:leadsLocal storageHolds a copy of forms you submitted on this demo build, so the confirmation page can show them back to you.Until you clear it

Both are strictly functional: they store a preference you set, on the device you set it on. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service you have requested does not require consent — which is why you have not been asked to click anything.

What we deliberately do not use

  • No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any other third-party tracker.
  • No advertising or remarketing cookies. We do not run ads.
  • No embedded content that sets cookies without asking — videos are self-hosted.
  • No cross-site fingerprinting, session recording or heat mapping.

We measure how the site is doing from anonymised server logs, which record the page requested, an approximate region and a truncated IP address, kept for 90 days. Those logs cannot identify you.

How to clear what is stored

  1. Open your browser settings and find "Privacy", "Site settings" or "Cookies and site data".
  2. Search for this site's address.
  3. Choose "Remove" or "Clear data".

Clearing it resets the theme to your system preference and empties the local copy of any form you submitted. Nothing else changes, and nothing breaks.

Payments

When a live payment provider is connected, its checkout may set its own strictly necessary cookies to process your transaction securely and prevent fraud. Those are governed by that provider's own policy, which we will link here at the point it goes live.

Changes

If we ever add anything that is not strictly necessary — and we have no plans to — we will ask for your consent first, and this page will say so before it happens.
