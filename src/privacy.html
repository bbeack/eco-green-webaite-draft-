1. Who we are

Rootstock Farm & Forest CIC ("Rootstock", "we", "us") is a community interest company registered in England & Wales, company number 11902847, at Cwm Aeron Farm, Hay-on-Wye, Powys HR3 5QA. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. Our data protection contact is privacy@rootstock.earth.

2. What we collect

We only ever collect data you type into a form on this site, plus a small amount of technical data your browser sends automatically.

  • Contact details — name, email address, and where you provide them, phone number and postal address.
  • Donation details — the amount, frequency, chosen project, any dedication message, and whether you have claimed Gift Aid. Card details are handled entirely by our payment provider and never reach our servers.
  • Volunteering details — the session you booked, group size and any access or dietary needs you tell us about. Health-related access needs are special category data and we keep them only until the session has taken place.
  • Partnership enquiries — company name, job context, and what you wrote in the message field.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and pages visited, in aggregate, for security and to understand which pages are useful.

We do not use tracking pixels, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics that build a profile of you across other websites. See our cookie policy.

4. Who we share it with

A short list, and it stays short:

  • Our payment provider, to take payments securely.
  • Our email provider, to send the updates you asked for.
  • HMRC, where you have claimed Gift Aid.
  • Our accountants and auditors, where required to verify our published figures.

Each of these acts as a processor under a written contract and may only use your data on our instructions. We do not transfer personal data outside the UK or EEA except where a provider is covered by adequacy regulations or equivalent safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

  • Donation records: seven years, as required by HMRC for Gift Aid.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe, plus 30 days.
  • Volunteer access and health information: deleted within 30 days of the session.
  • General enquiries: two years from your last contact with us.
  • Website server logs: 90 days.

6. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to be informed, of access, to rectification, to erasure, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to object — including an absolute right to object to direct marketing. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting anything we did before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@rootstock.earth. We respond within one month, usually within a week, and we do not charge for it.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to supporter data is limited to the three staff who need it, protected by multi-factor authentication, and reviewed every quarter. If a breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours and tell you directly without undue delay.

8. Complaints

Tell us first if you can — privacy@rootstock.earth — and we will try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change anything material we will say so on this page and, where the change affects how we use data you have already given us, email you about it. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
